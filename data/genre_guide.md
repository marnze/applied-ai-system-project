# TuneJudge Genre Guide

A second knowledge source for the RAG pipeline. When songs are retrieved from
`songs.csv`, the notes for their genres are retrieved from this file too, so the
AI can explain *why* a genre fits a listening situation instead of only quoting
numbers. Each section starts with `## <genre>` exactly as the genre is spelled
in `songs.csv`.

## pop
Catchy, polished songs built around a strong chorus. Works for upbeat moments,
getting ready, and background energy at a gathering. Usually electronic-leaning
production rather than acoustic.

## lofi
Low-fidelity, beat-driven instrumentals with soft drums and warm, slightly
dusty textures. Popular for studying, reading, and late-night work because it
stays in the background without lyrics pulling focus.

## rock
Guitar, bass, and drums with a driving rhythm. High-energy rock suits workouts,
driving, and moments when you want to feel pumped up.

## ambient
Slow, spacious sound with little or no beat. Good for sleep, meditation, and
deep focus. It is designed to fade into the background.

## jazz
Improvisation, swing rhythms, and rich chords, often on acoustic instruments
like piano, upright bass, and horns. Fits relaxed settings such as coffee shops,
dinners, and slow mornings.

## synthwave
Retro electronic music inspired by 1980s film soundtracks, with analog-style
synthesizers. Often moody and nocturnal, and a classic pick for night drives.

## indie pop
Pop songwriting with a lighter, more handmade feel than mainstream pop. Bright
and melodic, good for sunny afternoons and casual hangouts.

## hip hop
Rhythm-first music built on beats and rapped vocals. Confident hip hop works
for workouts, commutes, and getting motivated.

## classical
Orchestral and solo instrumental music, usually fully acoustic. Calm pieces suit
studying, reading, and winding down; dramatic pieces can be intense.

## country
Storytelling songs with guitars, fiddle, and twang, often about home, the road,
and the past. Fits road trips and nostalgic moods.

## edm
Electronic dance music with big drops and steady four-on-the-floor beats.
Built for parties, clubs, and high-intensity workouts.

## r&b
Smooth vocals over soulful grooves. Often romantic, fitting date nights and
slow evenings.

## metal
Heavily distorted guitars, fast drums, and aggressive vocals. The most intense
genre in the catalog, suited to heavy lifting and letting off steam.

## folk
Acoustic, lyric-focused songs, often stripped down to voice and guitar. Fits
quiet, reflective, or sad moments.

## reggae
Laid-back offbeat rhythms from Jamaica with warm, uplifting energy. Good for
beach days, sunshine, and relaxing with friends.

## latin
A broad family of styles (such as salsa, bachata, and reggaeton) with strong
dance rhythms. Often passionate and great for dancing.
